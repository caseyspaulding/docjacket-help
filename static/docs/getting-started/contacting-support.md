# Contacting Support

You can reach the DocJacket team from inside the app, and every reply comes back to the same place.

## Sending a support request

- **From Ask AI.** In the Ask AI panel, click **Send to support**. DocJacket drafts the request with the page you're on and an excerpt of the conversation. Review it, remove anything private, and click **Send request**.
- **From the Contact Support page.** On the **Your Support Requests** tab, type your question and click **Send request**.
- **Reporting a problem.** Open the **Help & Support** menu (the help icon in the top bar) and choose **Report a problem**. After you submit, DocJacket takes you to the **Support Tickets** tab so you can follow it.

## Where do I see the reply to my ticket or support request?

Replies land on the **Contact Support** page in DocJacket (`/help/contact`), not only in your email.

- **Replies to a message or support request** appear on the **Your Support Requests** tab as a conversation. You can reply right there.
- **Problems you reported with Report a problem** are listed on the **Support Tickets** tab, along with problems anyone else at your organization reported. Click a ticket to see its status, our responses, and the resolution once it's fixed.

You'll know a reply has arrived because:

- **A "Support replied" notice pops up** while you're using DocJacket, with no refresh needed. If your browser allows notifications and DocJacket is in a background tab, you also get a browser notification.
- **The sidebar shows a "Support replied (N)" link** while you have unread replies. Click it to open your support requests.
- **You also get an email** if you haven't read the reply in DocJacket within about 5 minutes. If you read it in the app first, no email is sent.

After you send a request, allow up to 24 hours for a reply.
