# Builder and Sections

The Front Door builder controls the public page visitors see. Use it to create the page layout, reorder sections, preview the page, publish or unpublish it, and track basic engagement.

## Starter templates

If your Front Door has no sections yet, click **Choose a starter template**. DocJacket can seed a starting layout for:

- TC
- Agent
- Broker
- Mix

Each starter shows the sections it will create before you continue. After the starter is created, you can edit, reorder, hide, or remove any section.

## Section blocks

Front Door sections are modular blocks. Current section types include:

- **Hero** — Logo, name, title, tagline, and background image (see [Hero image](#hero-image))
- **Primary CTA** — The main button you want visitors to click
- **Secondary actions** — Up to three smaller CTA buttons
- **Services** — Your public offerings catalog
- **Packages** — Bundled service packages
- **Testimonials** — Client quotes
- **Share card** — QR code, copy link, and vCard contact save
- **About** — Bio and photo
- **Contact** — Phone, text, email, social links, address, and map
- **Brokerage** — Brokerage name and logo
- **Credentials** — Designations and certifications
- **Service area** — Cities, counties, or zip codes you cover
- **Social proof** — Public stats such as transactions closed or years of experience
- **Active listings** — Listings you choose to feature
- **Recent closings** — Sold homes you choose to feature
- **Payment methods** — Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, PayPal, or Stripe Checkout links

## Editing layout

From the section list you can:

- Drag sections to reorder them
- Toggle a section visible or hidden
- Click a section to customize it
- Remove a section
- Add another section that is not already on the page

Hidden sections stay in the builder but do not appear on the public page.

## Hero image

Click the **Hero** section in the builder to open its editor. Under **Hero background image**, click **Upload image** (or paste an image URL), then click **Save**.

The hero has two styles, set by **Hero style**:

- **Portrait photo — full-bleed hero** — one large photo across the top of the page.
- **Cover band — cover strip + round avatar** — a short strip across the top with your headshot in a circle below it.

### Image size

Uploads can be JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF, up to 8 MB.

The page is about 640 px wide on a computer and about 440 px wide on a phone. How much of your image shows depends on **Image display**:

| Image display | Portrait hero height | Cover band strip height |
|---|---|---|
| **Compact band — crops more** | 300 px | 90 px |
| **Standard band — crops to fit** | 420 px | 120 px |
| **Tall band — crops less** | 560 px | 200 px |
| **Full image — no crop** | Your image's own shape | Your image's own shape |

The three band options fill a fixed height and crop your image from the center to fit. On a phone the page is narrower but the height stays the same, so a band crops more off the left and right sides.

**Automatic** uses **Standard band** when your logo or name and tagline show on the hero. When both are turned off, it uses **Full image**.

Recommended sizes:

- **Portrait, band options** — a landscape photo about **1280 × 840 px**. Keep faces and anything important near the center, because the edges get cropped, especially on phones.
- **Cover band, band options** — a wide image about **1280 × 240 px**, with nothing important near the top or bottom.
- **Full image — no crop** — any width of **1280 px or more**, at your design's own shape. The whole image shows on every screen size, scaled to fit the page width. Use this for a pre-made banner, such as one designed in Canva.

### Hero image looks darker at the top and bottom

DocJacket adds a dark shading over the hero photo so white text and icons on top of it stay readable. On the **Portrait photo** hero the shading is light at the very top, clear through the upper middle, and gets much darker toward the bottom. Any text that is part of your image, such as a tagline designed into a banner, gets darkened if it sits near the top or bottom edge.

On the **Portrait photo** hero, the shading appears whenever something sits on top of the photo:

- Your logo, when **Show organization logo on the hero** is checked
- Your name and tagline, when **Show name & tagline on the hero** is checked
- The round contact icons along the bottom of the hero. These come from the **Contact** section: your **Phone**, **Public email**, and any **Social links** you have filled in. They show on the hero even if the Contact section itself is hidden.

To show a banner that already has your name and tagline in it:

1. In the **Hero** editor, uncheck **Show organization logo on the hero**.
2. Uncheck **Show name & tagline on the hero**.
3. Set **Image display** to **Full image — no crop** so the banner shows whole.
4. Click **Save**.

If you have a phone number, public email, or social links in the **Contact** section, the hero still shows those icons, so the shading stays. Your phone number is used across DocJacket, including for text messages, so don't clear it just to remove the shading. Instead, place the text in your banner in the upper middle of the image, where the shading is clear, and keep it out of the bottom third.

The **Cover band** style always adds a light shading over its strip, whatever is turned on. If you want a banner to show as close to its original as possible, use the **Portrait photo** style.

## Previewing

The preview panel shows how the page will render. Use the device controls to check:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile
- Card

The **Card** view is the compact scan-optimized contact card used by QR-code sharing.

## Publishing

Use **Publish** when the page is ready. Until the page is published, visitors who open the public URL see that the page is not set up yet. The owner can still preview the draft.

After publishing, the page can be opened from **View site**. Use **Unpublish** if you need to take the page offline.

## Analytics and SEO

The builder includes:

- Page views
- Unique visitors
- CTA clicks
- QR scans
- vCard downloads
- Link copies
- Lead submits
- View-to-lead conversion
- CTA and lead activity by preset
- Traffic source
- Daily activity chart

You can also set the page title, meta description, and social share image used when the Front Door URL is shared.
