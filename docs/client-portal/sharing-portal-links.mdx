# Sharing Portal Links

Portal links are how clients, agents, and other transaction parties access their portal view. Each link is unique, secure, and managed individually.

There are two transaction-level places to work with portal links:

- **Portal tab** — Send portal links, preview the portal, manage branding, review analytics, and manage link details.
- **Contacts tab** — Manage each transaction contact's link from their contact card, including create, copy, regenerate, revoke, and send portal-link delivery options.

## Creating a portal link

Most of the time you don't need to create a link. **Send portal links** gives each person their own link and sends it. Create one yourself only when you want a link without sending it, an agent or vendor view, or a link that expires.

1. Open a transaction and go to the **Portal** tab
2. Click **More link options (agent view, vendor view, expiring link)** under the list of links
3. Choose the portal type: Buyer/Seller, Agent, or Vendor
4. Select a transaction contact
5. Set an expiration period: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or never
6. Click **Create Link**

If the person already has a Buyer/Seller link, the drawer doesn't make a second one. It shows **Copy their link** and **Resend their link** instead.

DocJacket generates a unique URL for that recipient. The Portal tab also shows a live preview so you can check the mobile or desktop view before sending.

You can also create a portal link directly from the transaction **Contacts** tab. On a contact card, click the portal-link action. DocJacket creates the link, copies it to your clipboard, and marks that contact as having an active portal link.

## What the link opens

The link opens a branded portal for that recipient. Depending on their access level and the data you've shared, the portal may include:

- Key dates and transaction status
- Portal-visible documents with preview and download controls when downloads are allowed
- Portal-visible tasks
- Parties, agents, title, lender, and service providers
- A live calendar subscription link
- Print and branded one-page PDF actions

Portal links are different from Disclosure Package links. Use a portal link when someone needs an ongoing transaction view. Use a Disclosure Package when someone needs to review a specific packet of documents and optionally acknowledge receipt.

## Sending the link

You can send the portal link by:

- **Email from DocJacket** — Open a link's details and send an invitation email right from DocJacket
- **Portal tab > Send portal links** — The one button in the **This deal's portal links** header. Select transaction contacts and choose email, text, or both for each one, or add email addresses by hand
- **Contacts tab > Send Portal Links** — Select transaction contacts and choose email, text, or both for each one
- **Copy and paste** — Copy the link URL and share it however you prefer (email, text, etc.)
- **Transaction Contacts tab** — Use each contact card to create, copy, regenerate, or revoke that contact's portal link

### Portal link emails come from your own mailbox

A portal link email you send always goes out from **your own connected Gmail or Outlook**, never from a `@docjacket.com` address and never from a teammate's mailbox. This is true for every organization, whatever the **Only send transaction email from a connected mailbox** setting says. If the deal is pinned to your mailbox, that mailbox sends; otherwise your default mailbox does. If the deal is pinned to a teammate's mailbox, your own mailbox sends.

If you have no connected mailbox, the email is not sent and nothing is created or changed. Send Portal Links, and the link's **Send via Email** option on the Portal tab, show:

> Connect your Gmail or Outlook account in Settings → Integrations to email portal links. You can still copy the link and send it yourself.

The **Send Agent Portal Link** dialog and **Regenerate portal link** on an agent contact show:

> Connect your Gmail or Outlook account to send the portal link from your mailbox.

Both messages have a **Connect your email** button that opens **Settings > Integrations** and brings you back to the page you were on once you connect. A refused regenerate leaves the agent's current link working. See [How Email Sending Works](../messages/how-email-sending-works.mdx).

Without a mailbox you can still copy a link and share it yourself, and text messages still send. If you ticked both email and text in Send Portal Links, the texts go and the result says no emails were sent. In the agent dialog, **Create link without emailing** still works.

Texts use your organization's SMS setup and safeguards. You don't need to create a link first. For each contact you text, DocJacket sends that contact's client portal link and creates one if they don't have it yet. Agent contacts get the deal's general client link. If a contact's link has been revoked or has expired, or the contact is set to Notify only, DocJacket skips that text and tells you why. To text a contact whose link is turned off, create a new link from their row on the **Contacts** tab, then send again.

### Who was already sent a link

The send window shows **Sent** and the date next to each person who already has a link. When you open it again, it ticks only people who haven't been sent one, so a second click doesn't email everyone again. If everyone has a link, nothing is ticked. Tick anyone to send theirs again. They get the same link, not a new one.

Until something on the deal has been sent, a line under the header reads "Not sent to anyone yet. Send it so your client can follow along."

The deal's shared client link, the one `{{portal_link}}` puts in emails, shows as a one-line note with **Copy** until it is used. Once it has been sent or opened it shows as a **Shared client link** card.

## Can portal links go out automatically?

DocJacket never sends a portal link on its own. For a new deal it prepares the send for you, and it goes out when you approve it.

### The Portal links ready card

When a new deal has a client or one of your agents who has not been sent a portal link, a **Portal links ready** card appears in your [Review queue](../operations/pending-review.md), usually within about 5 minutes.

- The card lists each person with their role, their email address, and which link they will get: **Client portal** or **Agent portal (all their deals)**.
- Everyone is ticked to start. Untick anyone you don't want to send to, then click **Send portal links**. Nothing is sent until you click it.
- A client gets their own client portal link, the same email **Send Portal Links** sends. An agent on your side gets their agent portal link.
- The emails go from your connected mailbox, so you need Gmail or Outlook connected in **Settings > Integrations**. Without one, the card reports that nothing was sent.
- An agent who already opens their agent portal is listed but not ticked, because the new deal already shows there.
- People are added to the card as you add them to the deal, until you send or dismiss it.

Who is listed:

- **Clients on your side**: the buyer on a deal where you represent the buyer, the seller where you represent the seller, both on a dual deal, and anyone in a custom client role.
- **Agents on your side** of the deal.
- The other side's buyer or seller, the cooperating agent, the lender and the title company are never listed. Use **Send Portal Links** for them.

When the card does not appear:

- The deal came from an import (CSV, Nekst, Open To Close, or monday).
- The deal is more than 72 hours old, or it is closed, cancelled or archived.
- Nobody on the deal qualifies yet, or everyone who does has no email address.
- A portal link on the deal has already been shared.

Each deal gets one card. If you send links from the deal yourself, the open card closes. **Dismiss** is final for that deal, and a card nobody answers goes away after 14 days. You can always use **Send Portal Links** afterwards.

To turn the card off for your organization, go to **Settings > Client Portal** and switch off **Prepare portal links for new deals**.

### Smart fields in a Playbook email

You can also put the **Client Portal Link** smart field, `{{portal_link}}`, in the email of a Playbook task.

- `{{portal_link}}` is the deal's shared Buyer/Seller link. DocJacket creates that link the first time the smart field is used, so you don't have to create it first.
- A task email that uses it follows the normal delivery rules. With **Require review before sending** on (the default), the email waits in your Review queue until you approve it. See [Email Automation](../tasks/email-automation.mdx#review-before-sending).
- `{{deal_agent_portal_link}}` sends your side's agent their own agent portal, where they follow all their deals with you. It is created the first time the smart field is used. It only goes to that agent: an email that also goes to a client or anyone else is stopped. It is empty on a deal with no side set or no agent with an email, and it stays empty if you revoked that agent's link. `{{portal_link}}` is never the Agent view, because the Agent view shows commissions, the TC fee, and lockbox details.
- To see whether the recipient opened it, check the Portal tab: each link shows its visit count and last access. Creating a general link also writes "Portal link created" to the Activity Log.

If you have a task such as "Send portal links", keep it as your reminder, or attach an email that uses `{{portal_link}}` to it.

## After you apply a Playbook

When you apply a Playbook to a deal that has no shared portal link and has a Client contact with an email address, the receipt offers **Send your client their portal**. It opens the Portal tab's send screen with only the Client contacts ticked, and it sends nothing by itself. You still choose who gets it and click send. The offer isn't shown to read-only seats or while you are impersonating another user.

## Managing links

From the transaction **Portal** tab, you can see active and inactive links for that transaction and:

- **View stats** — See how many times each link has been viewed
- **Extend** — Push back the expiry date
- **Revoke** — Immediately disable a link if you need to cut off access
- **Reactivate or delete** — Restore an expired/revoked link when appropriate. A link that was never sent and never opened has a **Delete** button. A link someone has opened can't be deleted. Revoke it instead; its history is kept
- **Preview** — Select a link to view the portal in the embedded mobile or desktop preview
- **Send by email** — Open a portal-link email composer from the link detail drawer
- **Review access details** — See last access time, last IP when available, calendar feed status, and revocation information

## Tips

- Each party gets their own link with appropriate visibility — buyers, sellers, and agents are different views
- Set expiration dates for sensitive transactions
- Check the stats to see if clients have actually viewed the portal — if they haven't, follow up
- Regenerate a contact's portal link when you need a new URL but want to keep portal access available
- Revoke a link when access should stop immediately
