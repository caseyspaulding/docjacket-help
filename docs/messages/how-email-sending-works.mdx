# How Email Sending Works

When DocJacket sends email from a transaction, it sends **through your connected
Gmail or Outlook account** — not from a generic DocJacket address. The message
goes out from your email address, a copy lands in your **Sent** folder, and any
reply comes back to **your inbox**, exactly as if you'd sent it yourself.

DocJacket only ever **sends** on your behalf. It does **not** read your inbox.

## Connect your mailbox first

Sending from your own address requires a connected mailbox. Connect Gmail or
Outlook from **Settings > Integrations**. See
[Google Workspace](../integrations/google-workspace.mdx) or
[Microsoft Outlook](../integrations/microsoft-outlook.mdx).

Until a mailbox is connected, manual sends from a deal prompt you to connect one,
and automated email falls back to DocJacket's own email service (a
`@docjacket.com` address) unless your organization requires a connected mailbox
(see below).

## Which mailbox an email sends from

Every transaction email sends from a specific person's connected mailbox,
depending on how it was sent:

| Email | Sends from |
|---|---|
| You compose, reply, or forward from a deal; approve a draft in the Review queue; or use **Review & send** | The mailbox chosen in the **From** field (see [Email you send by hand](#email-you-send-by-hand)) |
| Task emails, reminders, scheduled emails, portal link emails, and automation-rule emails | The deal's **assignee's default mailbox**. If the deal is unassigned, or the assignee has no connected mailbox, see [Requiring a connected mailbox](#requiring-a-connected-mailbox) |
| Transaction Summaries and other scheduled reports | The person who **created the schedule** |

If that person hasn't connected a mailbox, what happens depends on one organization setting:

- **Setting off (the default):** automated email goes out through DocJacket's email service from a `@docjacket.com` address instead.
- **Setting on** (**Only send transaction email from a connected mailbox**): it goes from the **account owner's** connected mailbox. If nobody has a connected mailbox, the email waits and the owner is nudged to connect one.

### Your default mailbox

Each login has **one default mailbox for sending**, shared across Gmail and
Outlook. It is marked **Default for sending** under **Settings > Integrations**
(open **Manage accounts** on the Gmail card, or look under the Outlook Mail
card), and you change it by selecting the radio button on a different mailbox.

- **Connecting another mailbox never changes your default.** Only choosing one does.
- **If your default needs reconnecting,** it stays your default. Your
  earliest-connected other mailbox sends in the meantime, and sending returns to
  your default as soon as you reconnect it.
- **If you remove your default mailbox,** the earliest-connected remaining
  mailbox becomes the default.

Automated email leaves from the **deal assignee's** default mailbox, so each
team member's own default decides how their deals' automated email looks to clients.

### Email you send by hand

On **Review & send**, the deal's **Email** tab (compose and reply), approving a
draft in the Review queue (including one-click approve), and the draft composer,
DocJacket picks the **From** mailbox in this order:

1. The mailbox you picked in the **From** field on this email
2. The deal's pin, if the pinned mailbox is yours (see below)
3. One of **your own** connected mailboxes whose address matches the deal assignee's mailbox
4. Your default mailbox

DocJacket only ever uses mailboxes connected to the login you are signed in
with, never a teammate's. If you change the **From** by hand, DocJacket keeps
your choice.

### Pinning a mailbox to a deal

When you have more than one mailbox connected, the composer shows a **Use this
mailbox for all email on this file** checkbox next to **From**. A deal has one
pin. Checking the box sets the deal's pin to the mailbox you selected, replacing
any earlier pin on that deal, including one a teammate set.

The pin only applies to the person who owns the pinned mailbox: automated email
for a deal assigned to them, and email they send by hand. For anyone else the
pin is ignored and the usual order applies. If you later move the dropdown to
another mailbox, the checkbox clears but the pin stays until you check it again
on the mailbox you want.

### When the From doesn't match the assignee

If the **From** isn't the deal assignee's mailbox, a note under it says who the
deal is assigned to and which address the email will go from, for example "This
deal is assigned to Sam (sam@example.com). This email will go from
pat@example.com." If the assignee's address is one of your own connected
mailboxes, the note also offers **Switch to** that address. One click and the
**From** changes. It never offers another member's mailbox.

## One person working deals under two logins

Some people work under two logins, for example a transaction coordinator login
and a personal agent login, each with its own mailbox. To make email go from the
right address on every deal:

1. On **each** login, go to **Settings > Integrations** and connect **both**
   mailboxes.
2. On each login, choose that login's **Default for sending**. Each login keeps
   its own default, and that default is what automated email from that login's
   deals uses.
3. Assign each deal to the login whose address should send its automated email.
4. When you send by hand on a deal, DocJacket starts on your connected copy of
   the assignee's mailbox, so the email goes out from the assignee's address.
   If you want a different address on one deal, change the **From**, or pin
   a mailbox to that deal.

If a deal is assigned to a login whose mailbox you haven't connected on the
login you're using, you'll see the note described above and your own default is used.

## When the sender has left your organization

Automated deal email never sends from someone who is no longer an active member of your organization, whether they were removed, deactivated, or moved to another organization. DocJacket treats them as having no connected mailbox, and their mailbox is left alone. Reassign their deals to an active member so automated email goes out from that person's mailbox.

What happens to an email for such a deal depends on the **Only send transaction email from a connected mailbox** setting (see [Requiring a connected mailbox](#requiring-a-connected-mailbox)):

- **Setting on:** the email goes from the account owner's connected mailbox. If the owner has none, the email is held and the owner is nudged to connect one.
- **Setting off (the default):** the email goes through DocJacket's email service, with the account owner as the Reply-To address, so replies reach the owner.

A one-off email that a departed member scheduled is not sent. It is marked failed with this message: "The person who scheduled this email is no longer an active member of this organization. Open the scheduled message and send it from your own mailbox."

## Where replies go

Because the email is sent from your real mailbox, replies go straight back to
**your inbox** in Gmail or Outlook — the normal way. You reply from your own inbox
like any other email. (Sent and received messages from a deal are also listed on
the transaction's **Email** tab for reference.)

Because DocJacket never reads your inbox, a reply that only went to Gmail or Outlook does **not** appear on the deal by itself. To file replies and their attachments on the deal, CC the deal's DocDrop address or auto-forward deal mail to your intake address — see [Getting Email Into DocJacket](./getting-email-into-docjacket.mdx).

## Requiring a connected mailbox

Organizations that want every deal email to come from a real mailbox — never a
`@docjacket.com` address — can turn on **Only send transaction email from a
connected mailbox** in **Settings > Organization**.

When this is on:

- Email sends from the sender's connected mailbox. If the sender hasn't
  connected one, automated email goes from the account owner's instead.
- If no mailbox is connected, a send you trigger yourself is **blocked** with a
  prompt to connect one, and an automated send is **held** until a mailbox is
  connected (the account owner is notified).

When it's off (the default), email still prefers a connected mailbox but falls
back to DocJacket's email service when none is available.

:::tip
Connect a mailbox for every team member who sends deal email, so their messages
always come from their own address and replies reach them directly.
:::

## Can you tell if a client opened an email?

Sometimes — and it depends on **which mailbox actually sent the message**, which is
the same distinction as the rest of this page. So no, connecting Gmail does not make
every message trackable; it does the opposite.

| How it was sent | Open tracking |
|---|---|
| From **your connected Gmail or Outlook** | **No.** DocJacket hands the message to your mailbox and it goes out as your own email. |
| From **DocJacket's email service** (no connected mailbox — the fallback described above) | **Yes.** DocJacket records when it was delivered and when it was first opened. |

When an email *is* tracked, an **Email opened** entry appears on the transaction's
activity timeline, and the message shows its delivery status.

This surprises people, so it's worth stating plainly: **connecting your mailbox
gives up open tracking.** That's not a setting you can toggle — it's a consequence
of the message being sent by Gmail or Outlook rather than by DocJacket. Once Gmail
sends it, it's an ordinary email from your account, and DocJacket has no more
visibility into it than into anything else you send from Gmail.

Most teams consider that the right trade. Sending from your own mailbox means better
deliverability, replies land in your inbox, and the message looks like it came from
you — which matters more on a deal than knowing whether someone opened it.

:::note What you can always see
Regardless of how a message was sent, the transaction's **Email** tab lists what was
sent and what came back. Replies file themselves onto the deal when you started the
thread from DocJacket — see
[Getting Email Into DocJacket](./getting-email-into-docjacket.mdx). Read receipts and
"has my client seen this" are the only things that depend on the sending path.
:::
